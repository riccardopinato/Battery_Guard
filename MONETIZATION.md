# Monetization — v1

Product ID: `battery_guard_pro_lifetime`

Type: Google Play non-consumable / one-time purchase.

## Free
- battery dashboard;
- threshold/temperature/cable alerts;
- foreground monitoring;
- widget + Quick Settings;
- charging history;
- 7-day Insights.

## Pro Lifetime
- Charge Doctor controlled tests and personal comparison;
- 30-day Insights.

## Principles
- no subscription in v1;
- no ads in v1;
- no artificial limitation of core safety/monitoring behavior;
- price comes from Google Play ProductDetails, never hardcoded in the app;
- restore purchases is always available when Play Billing is available.

## External configuration required
Create `battery_guard_pro_lifetime` in Play Console and activate it for the application before purchase verification can pass.
