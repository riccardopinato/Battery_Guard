# Battery Guard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Battery Guard is designed as a local-first utility.

## Data processed on the device
Battery Guard reads battery information exposed by Android, including charge level, charging status, temperature when available, voltage/current when available, charging source and power-save state. It can store local charging history, alerts, Charge Doctor tests and app preferences.

## Data sent to the developer
Battery Guard does not send battery telemetry, charging history, Charge Doctor results or app usage analytics to the developer. There is no Battery Guard account and no Battery Guard cloud backend in v1.

## Backup
Android Auto Backup is disabled for Battery Guard so that local battery history is not silently copied to cloud backup by the app configuration.

## Purchases
If Battery Guard Pro is purchased, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Battery Guard receives purchase/entitlement information required to unlock the non-consumable product. The developer does not receive battery telemetry as part of this process.

## Advertising and tracking
v1 contains no advertising SDK and no behavioural-tracking SDK.

## Deletion
Local history and Charge Doctor tests can be deleted from the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data under normal Android application-data semantics.

## Contact
A public support/privacy contact must be added before Play production rollout.
